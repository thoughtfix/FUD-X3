# Experimental Hardware & Responsible Use Disclaimer

FUD-X3 is passive counter-surveillance firmware. It listens for the Bluetooth
and Wi-Fi signatures of nearby devices and networks and reports what it sees. It
is receive-only: it does not transmit, join networks, deauthenticate, spoof, or
attack anything. Any scanning, logging, or monitoring made available by this
software, or by devices running it, is intended for lawful personal awareness
and educational purposes only.

be regulated in your jurisdiction. Do not use this software to stalk, harass,
track, profile, or otherwise unlawfully monitor any person, and do not use it to
